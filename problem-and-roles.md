Many students want internships and jobs, but the opportunities are spread across different websites, social media groups, college notices and messages. Because of this, students often miss important details and deadlines. They also keep their applications in notes or spreadsheets, so they forget where they applied and what the current status is. This problem mostly affects college students and fresh graduates who apply to many places at the same time.
Organisers and placement teams face a similar problem. They have no single, organised place to post opportunities and manage the students who apply.
CareerBridge is a web portal that solves this in one place. Students can create a profile with their skills, search and filter opportunities, apply, and track the status of every application. Admins can post and manage opportunities and update application status. As a result, students save time and do not miss deadlines, and admins can manage applications in a clear and organised way.
Student role: the main user who looks for opportunities and applies.
Register and log in
Create and update profile with skills
Browse, search and filter opportunities
View opportunity details
Apply to an opportunity
Track the status of each application
Admin role: the person who manages the portal.
Log in to the admin panel
Add a new opportunity
Edit, close or delete an opportunity
View applicants for each opportunity
Update application status
View statistics on the dashboar
